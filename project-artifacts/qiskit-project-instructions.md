**Florida Qiskit Fallfest Hackathon 2026 State Championships!**

**Date**: Saturday, Nov 14, 2026 12pm-3pm

**Location**: Florida Atlantic University, 777 Glades Rd. Boca Raton, FL 33431

**Description:** First-place winners from each of the participating universities will compete for the first ever Qiskit Fallfest Hackathon state final championships in Florida! Winners will receive award and prizes. 





Link to Florida Tech GitHub repo: https://github.com/robertloredo/Florida-Tech-Qiskit-Fallfest-Hackathon-2026





Step 0: Register/Sign in to your IBM Qiskit account so you can have access to run your circuit on an IBM Quantum computer: https://quantum.cloud.ibm.com/ 



Step 1: Follow the Qiskit "Hello World" instructions, which will help you install Qiskit (use your favorite IDE: VS Code, Google Collab, qBraid, etc.) save your account credentials, and run your first quantum circuit both locally and on a real quantum system here first: 

https://quantum.cloud.ibm.com/docs/en/guides/hello-world



**To create your teams and participate in the Qiskit Fallfest Hackathon:** 

Step 2: Create an issue in the Florida Tech Qiskit hackathon GitHub repo using the template below (You can also see an example issue in the ISSUES tab titled "Example-Team-Project" (https://github.com/robertloredo/Florida-Tech-Qiskit-Fallfest-Hackathon-2026/issues/1): 



1\. Team name:

2\. Team members: Full name, and edu email. 

3\. Team project title: 

4\. Overview of project: Describe the background of the problem (the challenge), the opportunity you believe that quantum might have a solution, and the benefit which it would provide. 

5\. Links to references, data sources, etc. that support your project.





Step 3: Once you have completed your project, simply upload your project code along with results, and the final version of you presentations as a comment to your project issue. You can update all artifacts until the deadline.





Step 4: Practice your presentation so that you can ensure that you present the challenge, opportunity, results, and benefit of your project to the judges within the allotted final presentation time. 





**NOTE**: The judge criteria (which will also be the same criteria for the state championship) is in the **judging-criteria.md** file in this repository.
